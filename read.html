Coffee House was founded in 2015 by two students, Harutyun and his friend. The first branch was opened near the Armenian-Russian University. After operating for a few months and making several mistakes, it was closed. Wanting to restart Coffee House, Harutyun, together with another friend, Narek, who had just returned from the army, reopened it by each investing 100,000 drams.

The foundation of the boys' activity is high human values, kindness, love, and respect. According to the boys, the goal should be very big, and money and success will come as a result. To this day, by following and maintaining these values, we have become the leading and largest take away coffee chain in Armenia.

Already 53 bench in the whole Armenia and outside...

It has already been 10 years since the founding, and we already have 53 branches, 250+ employees, and hundreds of thousands of happy customers.
